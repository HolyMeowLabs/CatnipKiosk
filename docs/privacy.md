# CatnipKiosk privacy policy

_Last updated: 6 October 2026_

CatnipKiosk shows one web page, chosen by the person who sets up the device, full screen on an Android TV or tablet.

## What CatnipKiosk collects

Nothing. CatnipKiosk has no accounts, analytics, advertising or crash reporting, and it does not send any information about you or your device to us or to anyone else.

## The web page you choose

CatnipKiosk loads the page you configure directly from that page's own server, using Android System WebView, the same component other Android apps use to show web content. What that site collects, and any cookies it sets, are governed by that site's own privacy policy. CatnipKiosk does not read or forward any of it.

## Settings

Your settings (the start page, navigation rules, display options and a salted hash of your admin PIN) are stored only on the device. They are excluded from Android backups and device-to-device transfer, and they are deleted when you uninstall the app.

## Permissions

- **Internet and network state**: to load your page and to retry when the network returns.
- **Run at startup**: so the kiosk can come back after the device restarts.
- **Device administration (optional)**: only if you set up hard lockdown yourself, using a command from a computer. It is used to keep the device on the kiosk page; it is not used to read your data. You can remove it at any time from CatnipKiosk's settings.

CatnipKiosk never asks for camera, microphone or location access, and it denies such requests from web pages.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
